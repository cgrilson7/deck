# Red fox vocalizations: archive sources (not Freesound / SFX sites)

13 files, all verified as real audio (`file` + `afinfo`; durations below come from afinfo). Every file was
characterized from a sox spectrogram (in `../spec/`) plus a 400–4000 Hz energy envelope (`../env.py`). I did not
listen to them: "sounds like" is read off the spectrogram. Timestamps are seconds into the ORIGINAL file.
All are redistributable: public domain / PD mark / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA / "free use for any purpose".
**Nothing here is NonCommercial.**

License notes:
- **PDM** = Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0. Radio Aporee uploaders apply it to their own recordings,
  so it works as a dedication. No attribution is required, but crediting the recordist is courteous.
- **CC BY-SA**: attribution is required, and a trimmed or edited clip must stay CC BY-SA. The app's own code
  is unaffected, because bundling a separate sound file is aggregation. Put the credits in an
  about/credits file.
- **CC BY**: attribution only.

Quick picks for alert sounds: **#1 NPS** (PD, clean yowls), **#6 XC961873** (CC0, rising bark
series), **#8 Calot** (PDM, clean scream), **#9 close fox** (PDM, sharp barks), **#10 garden** (CC BY,
single barks), **#12 Kilburn** (PDM, the only real gekkering).

### 1. `NPS-Yellowstone-YELLMJ23200837-redfox.mp3`
- **Sounds like:** four short hoarse yowls/barks (~0.3–0.5 s each, energy 0.7–2.5 kHz). Clean and quiet
  between calls (SNR ~44 dB). "A red fox emits a series of yowls during a quiet night."
- **Duration:** 8.69 s (mp3 192k, 44.1 kHz stereo)
- **Source page:** https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/sounds-redfox.htm
- **Direct file:** https://www.nps.gov/nps-audiovideo/legacy/mp3/imr/avElement/yell-YELLMJ23200837redfox.mp3
- **Author:** NPS / Shan Burson (Yellowstone National Park Sound Library, created 2008-03-07)
- **License:** Public domain (a work of a US federal employee)
- **Attribution:** none required. Courtesy: "Red fox, NPS / Shan Burson, Yellowstone National Park Sound Library"
- **Trim:** it can be used whole. Single calls: 0.60–1.50, 2.35–2.90, 4.20–4.90, 7.60–8.45.

- **Source page:** https://xeno-canto.org/961873

### 9. `aporee_19236_22327-close-fox-rain.mp3`: best sharp barks
- **Sounds like:** a close, agitated young fox. It gives dozens of sharp, clear, harmonic "yap/wow" barks (~0.3–0.4 s,
  fundamental ~0.9 kHz, very loud at −8 dBFS) every ~2.5 s over light rain. The recordist says "he was not happy".
  An aircraft passes at points (the Heathrow flight path).
- **Duration:** 198.09 s (mp3, 44.1 kHz stereo)
- **Source page:** https://archive.org/details/aporee_19236_22327 (Radio Aporee, "Close Fox in the rain under the Flightpath", Hither Green, London)
- **Direct file:** https://archive.org/download/aporee_19236_22327/closefoxplanerain.mp3
- **Author:** Peter Cusack (2013-08-14)
- **License:** Public Domain Mark 1.0
- **Attribution:** none required. Courtesy: Peter Cusack, radio aporee
- **Trim:** 0.50–1.60 and 5.30–6.30 (clean singles). Avoid 2.3–3.9, where a second, higher pulsed call
  (possibly a bird) overlaps. Later barks sit at 10.1, 12.3, 14.8, 23.3, 25.7, 38.5, 60.1 and 94.6 s (each ~0.4 s).

## Rejected (moved to `../rejected/` or never downloaded)
- **xeno-canto, 392 of its 396 red fox recordings:** CC BY-NC-SA (342), BY-NC-ND (49) or BY-NC (1), so
  NonCommercial. Only 4 were not NC: #6 and #7 (kept), and XC1004912 and XC962669 (below).
- **XC1004912** (CC BY 4.0, cubs' social call): almost inaudible (peaks around −57 dBFS).
- **XC962669** (CC0): faint, and the recordist suspects a captive bird.
- **Commons "Vulpes vulpes, at Henclová" (XC108315, CC BY-SA 4.0):** a faint, distant pulsed series (SNR ~13 dB).
- **Internet Archive "HAMBIO" red fox clips** (Hamilton Naturalists' Club, CC BY 4.0, 11 files): distant
  autonomous-recorder captures, all SNR ≤14 dB, with nothing usable above the noise.
- **Other Radio Aporee items not taken:** Peter Cusack's dawn chorus with fox barks (10 min, birds dominate),
  "Strange fox in the stormy dawn" (the recordist is unsure it is a fox), distant fox/owl recordings, "fox chase" (PD,
  9 min, not auditioned), the Cork pond recording (egrets/herons dominate), and "Fox barking and sirens" (sirens).
  Also Estonia/Slovakia/Poland fox items, which are BY-NC-ND.
- **Macaulay Library (Cornell):** media is © Cornell Lab, and reuse needs a licence request. Not redistributable,
  so nothing was downloaded.
- **USFWS:** the old digitalmedia.fws.gov library now redirects to an image search. No red fox audio found.
- **Other NPS pages:** Yellowstone's library has only the one red fox clip (#1).
- **Wikimedia Commons:** only 5 real fox recordings exist in "Audio files of Vulpes vulpes". The rest are
  pronunciations and songs.
