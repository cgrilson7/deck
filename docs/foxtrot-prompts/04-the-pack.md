# The Pack

## Prompt

Use the attached pixel-art fox sprite sheet as the character reference for Foxtrot.

Foxtrot, a voxel fox built from chunky cubes — a 16x16 pixel-art sprite extruded faithfully into 3D. Rust-orange body, lighter orange highlight blocks on the back and cheeks, cream-white muzzle, chest bib and tail tip, a grey-white band before the tail tip, thick dark charcoal outline cubes on every edge (not pure black), a single white cube for the eye, dark stubby feet. Hard cube edges, no smoothing, no fur texture. Warm limited palette: rust, amber, cream, charcoal, grey.

He is the alpha. He sits elevated on a broad rocky outcrop at the crest of a hillside at golden hour, alert pose — upright, ears straight up, tail raised, looking calmly down over the field below. The light catches the cubes of his back and ear tips in warm amber.

Below and ahead of him, spread across the slope, five gold voxel foxes — the betas — are at work. Same blocky shape as the alpha but warm gold-yellow where his body is rust-orange, their outlines the same dark charcoal, their eyes the same single white cube. Each glows faintly from within, a soft gold luminance on the grass around its feet. One sprints mid-stride across the middle distance. One digs at a burrow mouth, dirt cubes scattering. One carries a small object in its jaws, heading uphill. One sits studying something on the ground. The fifth has paused and turned back to look up at the alpha, ears forward, waiting for a signal.

Near the alpha's head, a blocky pixel-font speech burst floats: "ARF!" in cream on charcoal, like a retro game bark — his acknowledgment to the one looking back.

The hillside is natural but the light is cinematic: long golden-hour shadows, warm amber washing the west-facing cubes, cool blue-grey on the east sides. Shallow depth of field — the nearest gold fox is sharp, the farthest softens. The sky is deep amber fading to violet at the zenith.

Panoramic composition, 16:9 aspect ratio. 2K resolution (2560x1440). Voxel-art characters in a semi-stylized painterly environment, cinematic golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field.

## Notes

- **Gold coat swap:** the betas must be warm gold-yellow, not orange. If the model makes them identical to the alpha, add "the five smaller foxes are distinctly gold-yellow, NOT rust-orange — they contrast clearly with the alpha's rust coat" near the top.
- **Voxel drift:** if the model softens toward a realistic fox or rounds the cubes, prepend "no fur, no realistic animal features, strictly cubic voxel construction, Minecraft-adjacent blocky style" to the base description.
- **Scale and hierarchy:** the alpha should read as slightly larger or more prominently placed than any single beta. If the model flattens the elevation, emphasize "the alpha is ABOVE the others, looking DOWN — a clear vertical separation between the ridge and the working field."
