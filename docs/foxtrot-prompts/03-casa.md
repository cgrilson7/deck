# Casa — guardian of the smart home

## Prompt

Use the attached pixel-art fox sprite sheet as the character reference for Foxtrot.

Cinematic 16:9 composition, a quiet living room at dusk. A real Apple Mac mini M2 — silver aluminium, rounded corners, thin profile — sits on a low wooden shelf against a cream wall, its front LED glowing soft amber. Perched on top of the mini is Foxtrot, a voxel fox built from chunky cubes: rust-orange body with lighter orange highlight blocks on the back and cheeks, cream-white muzzle, chest bib and tail tip, a grey-white band before the tail tip, thick dark charcoal outline cubes defining every edge, a single white cube for the eye, dark stubby feet. He is a holographic projection — translucent glowing cubes with faint amber edge light, a subtle scanline shimmer, casting a soft warm glow on the aluminium beneath him. Hard cube edges, no smoothing, no fur. Proportions of a small dog.

He is in his alert pose: sitting up tall, ears pricked straight up, head turned sharply toward the front door across the room. He has just heard something. A blocky speech burst floats beside his head — "CHRRP!" in chunky pixel font (Press Start 2P), cream text on a charcoal pill, like a retro game bark.

The room is warm and modern: hardwood floor, a cream wool rug, warm table lamps casting amber pools, a window on the far wall with the last blue of evening outside. Near the shelf, two or three small translucent frames float in the air like AR overlays — camera feeds showing a front porch, a hallway, a gate — faintly glowing, slightly ghosted, the same holographic quality as the fox. The front door is visible in the middle distance, the source of whatever he heard.

Photoreal environment, only the fox, the speech burst, and the camera frames are digital-holographic. Shallow depth of field centred on Foxtrot and the mini. Cinematic lighting: cool blue dusk through the window, warm amber lamps inside. 4K resolution, voxel art character in a photorealistic setting.

## Notes

- If the model drifts toward a realistic fox or a plush toy, reinforce early: "no fur, no realistic animal, strictly extruded cubes, Minecraft-adjacent voxel style, faithful to the sprite sheet."
- The Mac mini must read as the real Apple hardware — silver, not space grey, with the rounded square profile of the M2. If it renders as a generic box, specify "Apple Mac mini M2, 2023 industrial design."
- The holographic treatment (translucent cubes, scanline shimmer, amber edge glow, light cast on surfaces) is what sells the idea that Foxtrot lives inside the mini and is projected out of it. If the model makes him solid, add "translucent like a hologram, the shelf visible faintly through his body."
