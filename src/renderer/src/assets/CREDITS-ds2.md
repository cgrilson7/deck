# ds2.webp

The DualShock 2 photo behind the PS2 tile's "Foxtrot Plays" pad (components/FoxtrotPlays.tsx).

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PlayStation2-DualShock2.png
- Author: Evan-Amos (own work, 14 December 2010)
- License: public domain (no attribution required; credited anyway)
- Changes: scaled from 3040×2440 to 1520×1220 and encoded as WebP (alpha kept).
- The cable is erased (a flood fill from its top end, then trimmed along the body's top edge), since the
  pad stands for a wireless controller and the cable ran where the R1 / R2 tabs go.
